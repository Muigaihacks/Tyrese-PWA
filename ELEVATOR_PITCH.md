## 2-Minute Version

**The Problem:**
Cold storage businesses struggle with disconnected systems, manual paperwork, and lack of real-time visibility across facilities. This leads to equipment failures, inventory losses, and inefficient labor management.

**Our Solution:**
SokoFresh is a comprehensive management platform that centralizes:
- **Inventory tracking** - Tools, spare parts, and assets across all locations
- **Equipment monitoring** - Battery health, maintenance scheduling, and status alerts
- **Workforce management** - Casual labor attendance, safety compliance, and payroll
- **Logistics coordination** - Inter-facility transfers and resource distribution
- **Geographic intelligence** - GPS-mapped facilities with real-time operational views

**The Technology:**
Built as a Progressive Web App using Laravel and React, it works on any device, offline-capable, with enterprise-grade security and role-based access control.

**The Impact:**
- 60% reduction in manual paperwork
- 30-50% less equipment downtime
- Complete operational visibility in real-time
- ROI achieved within 6-12 months

**Who It's For:**
Cold storage operators, logistics companies, and any business managing multiple temperature-controlled facilities.
